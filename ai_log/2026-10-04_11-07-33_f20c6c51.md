## AI Summary

The idea works: a point plus distance falloff gives you partial credit the way a geography teacher would grade, and fractions keep it independent of the image. But several parts are underspecified, and a few will break as written. Here they are, most serious first.

## What breaks as written

**1. Fractions aren't a distance unit.** A radius of 0.05 is 5% of the width horizontally but 5% of the height vertically. A US map is roughly 1.6:1, so a "circle" in fraction space becomes a vertical ellipse and players get more slack north–south than east–west. The fix is to store the image's aspect ratio with the question and correct for it:

```
aspect = config.imageWidth / config.imageHeight      // captured when authored
dx = (click.x - target.x)
dy = (click.y - target.y) / aspect                   // now both in "image-width units"
d  = sqrt(dx² + dy²)
```
