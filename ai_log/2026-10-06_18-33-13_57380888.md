## Draft design

**One type, not two modes.** A spectrum question is just a slider from 0 to 100 with step 1, words at the ends, and the numbers hidden. So the backend only ever deals with numbers, and "spectrum" is a preset in the editor. Working name: **`slider`**. It describes the interaction for both uses better than `closest_guess` does.

**Config (the question's settings, in the existing JSON column, so no migration):**
- `min`, `max`, `step` (all numbers);
- optional `unit` (e.g. "hot dogs", "$");
- optional `minLabel` and `maxLabel` (e.g. "Ice cold" and "Lava"), which replace the end numbers;
- `showValue` (yes/no): whether the phone shows the live number as you drag. The spectrum preset turns it off.
