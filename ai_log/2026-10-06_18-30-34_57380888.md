## AI Summary

The constraint holds up well: every idea below scores each player's answer alone, as the current scoring code does. One thing makes this easier: **the canvas requirement is already met by `map_pin`**, so the party type can use whatever interaction suits a party best.

The pattern behind good party questions is that the *projector reveal* is the social moment, while scoring stays individual. People laugh at how wrong the room was, not at a shared score.

## Ideas

**1. Estimation: "Closest guess"** (e.g. "How many hot dogs has Joey Chestnut eaten in one contest?")
- **Phone:** a number pad.
- **Scoring:** partial credit by how far off you were. Full points within X%, falling to zero at Y%. This is the Guess-the-Correlation model from the instructions.
- **Reveal:** every guess plotted on a number line with the true answer dropped in. Classic Wits & Wagers energy, minus the betting.

**2. Ordering: "Rank it / Timeline"** (e.g. "Order these movies by box office", "Oldest to newest meme")
